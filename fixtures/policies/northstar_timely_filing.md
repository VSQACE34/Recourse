# Northstar Advantage — Claims Submission and Appeals (Chapter 3)

- Initial claims must be received within 120 days of the date of service.
- Provider appeals must be filed within 60 calendar days of the date on the remittance advice.
- Timely-filing denials are overturned only with documentary proof of submission within the
  120-day window.
- Appeals: appeals@northstaradv.example
