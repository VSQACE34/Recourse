# Meridian Health Plan — Modifier 25 and Same-Day E/M Services (Provider Manual §9.2)

An evaluation and management (E/M) service reported on the same day as a minor procedure
(0- or 10-day global) is bundled into the procedure payment UNLESS the E/M is significant and
separately identifiable. To be separately payable with modifier 25, the medical record must show:
1. A problem addressed that is distinct from the problem for which the procedure was performed; AND
2. History, examination and medical decision-making for that distinct problem, beyond the
   pre-procedure evaluation inherent to the procedure; AND
3. The E/M documentation must stand on its own if the procedure note were removed.

Denials under CARC 97 may be appealed with the full visit note demonstrating the above.
