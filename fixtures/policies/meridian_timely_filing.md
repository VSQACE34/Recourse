# Meridian Health Plan — Timely Filing and Appeals (Provider Manual §4)

- Initial claims must be received within 90 days of the date of service.
- Claims denied for timely filing (CARC 29) may be appealed within 180 days of the denial date
  ONLY with proof of timely filing: a clearinghouse acceptance report or Meridian acknowledgement
  showing receipt within the 90-day window.
- Claims first submitted after the 90-day window without such proof are not eligible for appeal
  and may not be billed to the member.
- All appeals: submit to provider.appeals@meridianhp.example with claim number, member ID,
  date of service and supporting documentation.
