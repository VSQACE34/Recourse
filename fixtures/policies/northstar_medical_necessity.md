# Northstar Advantage — Coverage Determinations (Chapter 5)

## 5.11 Electrocardiogram (CPT 93000)
Covered when ordered for evaluation of symptoms suggestive of cardiac disease including chest
pain, palpitations, syncope or unexplained dyspnoea, or for monitoring of established cardiac
conditions. Screening ECG without symptoms or documented risk factors is not covered unless
part of the Welcome to Medicare visit.

## 5.4 Advanced Imaging of the Spine
Requires 6 weeks of conservative management and neurological findings or red flags, consistent
with national guidelines.
