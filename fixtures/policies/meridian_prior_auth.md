# Meridian Health Plan — Prior Authorisation Requirements (Provider Manual §5)

## 5.1 Services requiring prior authorisation
Advanced imaging (CT, MRI, PET), elective inpatient admissions, and outpatient surgery require prior
authorisation. Claims submitted without an authorisation number are denied with CARC 197.

## 5.4 Emergency exception
Prior authorisation is NOT required when the service is provided on an emergency basis, defined as a
condition that a prudent layperson would believe requires immediate attention to avoid serious jeopardy
to health. The record must document ALL of:
1. the presenting condition and why immediate imaging or treatment was necessary;
2. that the service was performed at the time of the emergency encounter (same visit); AND
3. a statement that obtaining authorisation would have caused an unsafe delay.
Claims denied under 197 that meet §5.4 may be appealed with the encounter documentation.

## 5.5 Retrospective authorisation
For non-emergency services, a retrospective authorisation request may be submitted within 14 calendar
days of the date of service. After 14 days the denial stands and the member may not be billed.
